The PROPHECY Study: T2D Multiomics

  • Mortality rate is ~10x higher
  • Age of onset is earlier
  • Complication rates \(\uparrow\uparrow\uparrow\)
    • Cardiovascular Disease
    • Chronic Kidney Disease
    • Diabetic Retinopathy
  • PROPHECY: 1391 from SA Aboriginal communities
  • DNA, DNAme, RNA, Proteomics, Lipidomics, Metabolomics, ECG, Retinal scans etc

Variation With The Indigenous Australian Population

  • 25% of genomic variation unique to Australian Aboriginal population
  • STARconsensus has shown improved mapping and quantification (Kaminow et al. 2022)
    • Even the wrong population group
  • If variants are driving progression \(\implies\) will we see it in the RNA-seq?
  • Can we bring the reference transcriptome closer to our participants?
  • What impact will this have at the gene-level and transcript-level?

PROPHECY Consensus Variants

  • Consensus variants: AF > 0.5 in ‘unrelated’
    • PROPHECY participants
    • AFR & Panhuman variant sets from 1000GP
  • Pilot Study of 6 participants: personalised (haploid) references
  • Tested using salmon (Srivastava et al. 2020)
  • Full DTE in sub-cohort of n=93

The Package transmogR

  • Can take a set of variants (SNP + INDEL)
    • Also checks for overlapping variants
  • Modify genome sequence \(\implies\) co-ordinates change
  • Track coordinate shifts after modification
  • Optional tags added to sequence IDs
  • Modify transcriptome sequences
  • digestSalmon(): parse counts, TPM, effLen & overdispersions
    • Can handle divergent references: chrY-excluded vs complete
    • Personalised transcriptomes \(\implies\) statistical assumptions?

Benchmarking

  • Very similar strategy to STARconsensus
  • Different assessment criteria (i.e. not unique-alignments)
  • Assumed that personalised references would be optimal quantification
    • n = 6 (Pilot Study)
  • “Ground truth” for comparison to consensus references
  • Chose all homogeneous sites + 50% of hetergeneous sites
    • Still only a haploid reference
  • Compared 1) GRCh38, 2) AFR, 3) ‘panhuman’ and PROPHECY based transcriptome references
    • panhuman derived from all 1000GP sub-populations
    • AFR most divergent from panhuman

Comparison Against Alternate References

  • Library sizes: ~50m (2x150)
  • PROPHECY consensus consistently closer to personalised
  • GRCh38 consistently furthest
  • Very similar to STARconsensus paper
